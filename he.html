Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

Sign in to your NoirAura account and enter your Order ID or tracking number above. For privacy, only orders saved to the current account can be shown.

What if I receive the wrong or damaged item?

We're sorry to hear that! Please contact our support team within 7 days of receiving your order with photos of the issue. We'll arrange a return and send you a replacement or issue a refund.

How can I cancel or modify my order?

You can modify or cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it. After that, the order enters our processing system and cannot be changed. Contact us immediately if you need to make changes.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for larger orders. All payments are processed securely through encrypted channels.

How long will shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping (2-3 business days) is available for an additional fee. International orders may take 10-14 business days depending on the destination.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by location. You'll see the exact cost at checkout before completing your purchase.

How can I contact NoirAura Support?

You can reach our support team via email at support@noiraura.com or through our contact form. Our team typically responds within 24 hours during business days.

Need More Help?

Our dedicated support team is here to assist you with any questions or concerns.
